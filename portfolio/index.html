PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your business records deserve careful protection.

Security is not just a badge on a website. It depends on the app's actual implementation, safe configuration, updates and responsible handling of information.

🛡️

Security-minded development with KAIxGenShop™ Protection

KAIxGenShop™ is the AI and protection identity associated with this project. The intended approach is to reduce unnecessary data handling, protect sensitive records, use secure communication and address security issues through ongoing maintenance.


Continuous protection depends on deployed security controls, monitoring availability, maintenance and timely response. No system can honestly promise absolute immunity from every possible threat.

✓

Data minimisation

The product aims to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Information required for a feature should be explained before use.

✓

Secure local storage

Sensitive local records should use appropriate platform security controls. Protection depends on the actual storage implementation and device.

✓

Protected communication

Network features should use HTTPS/TLS, with appropriate authentication, access control and secure handling of credentials.

✓

Responsible access control

Financial records should only be accessible to authorised users. Permissions and account protection depend on the enabled features.

✓

Transparent data practices

Users should be informed about what information is stored, why it is needed, where it is processed, and how deletion or export works.

✓

Financial information safety

DukaanSaathi AiGen is a business-record management concept. Never share UPI PINs, bank passwords, OTPs or card security codes with anyone.

Important privacy notice: Do not publish an absolute “we collect no data” claim until all app code, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, AI integrations and third-party SDKs have been audited. Local business records, if saved, are still data processing. Publish a complete privacy policy that matches the actual released app.
FINANCIAL LITERACY

Samajhdaar hisaab. Behtar business decisions.

Helping shopkeepers understand the numbers behind everyday business, not just record transactions.

📘 Financial knowledge for every dukandaar

The product vision includes practical guidance to help users distinguish sales, expenses, profit, cash flow, stock costs and outstanding customer payments. Clear records can support better decisions, but they cannot guarantee business profits.

💰

Sales vs Profit

Learn why total sales are not the same as profit. Product costs, expenses, returns and applicable taxes can all affect the final result.

🧮

Expense Management

Record shop rent, electricity, transport and other expenses to understand the cost of running the business.

📒

Responsible Credit

Track customer udhar and supplier dues to understand outstanding payments and manage working capital.
